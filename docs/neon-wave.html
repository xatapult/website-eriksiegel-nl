Neon Wave 

The "Neon Wave" is a rather weird contraption that was used in our 2026 stage production (amateur theater group Quasi playing "Not just Freaky", written and directed by Tanja Mensink, March/April 2026)

It features: a flip-up screen displaying text, a robotic arm that examines the audience's faces and then sprays a mood-matching scent into the crowd. Everything is, of course, fake and supposedly AI-driven.

In the same play, another device of me was used: the Freaky stage.
